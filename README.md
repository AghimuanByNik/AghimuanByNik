### 🧠 About Me
I am a Computer Science student specializing in Software Engineering at MSEUF - Lucena City. Driven by a genuine fascination with how AI solves complex problems, I love bringing ideas to life through development. Whether I'm building a tri-stream deep learning model for digital forensics, engineering a RAG chatbot, or developing an offline-first full-stack platform, I focus on building intelligent, impactful systems.

### 🔭 What I'm Building
*   **KILATIS (Research):** Developing a tri-stream deep learning architecture with explainable forensic reporting for blind image tamper detection.
*   **Library Copilot:** Refining a RAG-based chatbot using Llama 3.1 and PostgreSQL (pgvector) with deterministic tool-calling.
*   **EULibrary:** An fffline-First, full-stack platform built to manage, search, and serve academic resources.
